- Open the Inventory app
- Create the replenishment rule for a product with a critical order date (delivery), set the minimum and maximum quantities
- Set the route to "Buy", assign a vendor
- Under Operations menu click "Calculate Critical Forecast"
- Under Reporting menu open the Critical Forecast
- The Critical Forecast by default shows products with a critical order date
- If the forecasted quantity for a product is less than the minimum quantity, the reorder button will appear on the forecast and a purchase order can be generated by clicking it
- The truck icon is visible in the Critical Forecast and the replenishment screen can be accessed from there
