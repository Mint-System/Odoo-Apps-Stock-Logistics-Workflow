Inventory Summary:

- Open Inventory > Overview > Incoming
- Pick an unconfirmed delivery and validate it
- Open Inventory > Operations > Run Inventory Summary to update the inventory summary report
- Open Inventory > Reporting > Inventory Summary and check whether the product from the validated delivery is shown in the inventory summary

Inventory Summary History:
- Open Inventory > Reporting > Locations
- Press Inventory at Date button and select a date
- Open product Desk Stand with Screen and replenish it with 125 units, validate the delivery
- Run the Inventory Summary action with current date and open the Inventory Summary, the new quantity for the product is shown
- Go back to the Locations and set the Inventory at Date before the date of delivery of the product
- Run the Invenotry Summary Action again for the same date, open the Inventory Summary and the product quantity will be updated to the date before the delivery
